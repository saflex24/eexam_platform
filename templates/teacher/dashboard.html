Student Reports — Complete Guide
What are Student Reports?

Professional, print-ready exam reports including school branding, student details, performance summary, and per-question analysis.

  • School logo, name & contact details
  • Student name, admission number & class
  • Marks, percentage, grade & pass/fail status
  • Detailed MCQ and theory question breakdown
How to Generate Reports
  1. Find the exam in your dashboard
  2. Click the button
  3. View all students who took the exam
  4. Download individual reports or bulk ZIP
Printing Tips
  • Optimised for Letter / A4, max 4 pages per student
  • Works colour or black & white
  • Use duplex printing to save paper
Customise School Branding
  1. Edit get_school_settings() in routes/teacher.py
  2. Update name, address, phone, email
  3. Place logo at static/uploads/school_logo.png
